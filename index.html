About

Quality professional working at the intersection of regulated industry and data.

I have over two years of hands-on GMP experience in pharmaceutical manufacturing, covering batch documentation review, in-process quality oversight, deviation management, CAPA, change control, and audit readiness. Alongside that I hold an MSc in Business Analytics from Aston University and a BSc in Biotechnology — a combination that means I can both hold the line on compliance and quantify what the quality system is actually doing.

That second part is what this portfolio demonstrates. Quality functions generate a great deal of data — deviations, closure times, audit findings, recurrence rates — and most of it is reported rather than analysed. The projects below apply analytical methods to regulatory and quality data in both pharmaceutical and financial services contexts, because the underlying discipline transfers: define the control, evidence that it operates, investigate honestly when it fails, and fix the cause rather than the symptom.

Analysis of 17,816 FDA drug enforcement records, with 3,000 recent recalls categorised by root cause. Finds CGMP deviations and contamination together account for 71% of recalls, and that 99.6% of recalls are firm-initiated rather than FDA-mandated.
